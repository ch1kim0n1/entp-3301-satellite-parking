The GAIA Drone Team is composed of Federal Avaiation Administration (FAA) licensed Remote sUAS Pilots under Part 107. The team has access to high end unmanned aerial vehicle systems that are on the bleeding edge of drone technology. The team prides itself in safety, meticulous flight planning, and accurate data collection.

UAVs bring new oppurtunities to GIS & Remote Sensning in terms of volume and velocity of data collection, variety of data sensor payloads, and oppurtunities for veracity of data.

The GAIA Drone Team is constantly looking for collobarotive efforts to apply this wonderful technology. Some of the newest payloads to the GDT include air quality monitoring station “Scentroid” DR1000, and a thermal infared sensor “FLIR Vue Pro”. GAIA Lab looks to begin a new air quality monitoring project on campus, and the GDT UAV collection will be a main contribution.

Below is a list of equipment the GDT currently has in its inventory. Note: not all equipment is listed below, the GDT has a full equipment inventory to run an UAV operation safely, effectively, and effeciently.
